Format as tools/casegoods/decors.md: name — collections and roles — swatch / source page (crop) — character — direction; provisional colour.

## Carcass and upholstery

- «Дуб Трюфельный» — Бритиш Бум (body of british-bum-krem-tryufel: carcass, tops, plinths, desk tops, bed frames, the ЛДСП backs of 1.03 / 2.18 / the loft's rack) — p. 131 (swatch «Каркас», `catpage.py 131 --swatch 0.755,0.85,0.81,0.89` → #786657, spread ±13.5 = a decor) — grey-brown «truffle» oak: light grey streaks over mid brown, long straight fibres with sawn cross-marks (the Sonoma-truffle look on the site photos), few cathedrals, matt — vertical on sides and wardrobe panels, x on tops, bottoms, plinths, bed boards; provisional #786657.
- «Крем» (fronts) — plain, no texture needed: p. 131 swatch «Фасад», `--swatch 0.68,0.85,0.73,0.89` → #e0d6cd (flat ±0.5). The same colour is used provisionally for the ХДФ backs (they photograph cream in the niches of 1.01, 2.17, 1.09).
- Upholstery of 1.34 (buttoned back cushion), grey-beige, unnamed — role `fabric` — p. 131 cut-out 1.34 — plain velour / matting, button tufting — provisional #9e958e.
- Upholstery of 1.32 (buttoned headboard panel), light warm grey, unnamed — role `fabric_light` — p. 131 cut-out 1.32 and the p. 131 interior — plain velour / matting — provisional #bfbbb2.

## Printed fronts («Рисунок на фасаде методом цветной фотопечати», p. 131)

Library materials `british_bum_<motif>`, used as a front's `print` (the picture is fitted to the whole +z face of the front: u across x, v up y; **up = +y**, the top of the picture is the front's top edge). They do not exist yet: the engine throws «нет материала печати» until the texture wave makes them. Common character: brown / terracotta hand-drawn line art (≈ 1.5–3 mm lines, some areas filled flat terracotta) on the «Крем» ground #e0d6cd. The source crops are `tools/casegoods/gen/prints/british-bum/<motif>.png`, rectified to the front's outline (a homography of the four corners read on the source); they still show the handle (paint it out; handle positions are in the designs) and the photo's shading.
