- «Дуб Бордо лайт 380» — Сорренто (body and front of sorrento-bordo-monastyr: carcass, fronts, coupe doors, headboard, mirror board); the same decor as Агата's «Дуб Бордо лайт 380 SWN» (agata-bordo-light) — no swatch on p. 113; the colour is Агата's swatch p. 27 (crop 0.675,0.865,0.73,0.905); photos p. 113 and the site (8C3A1962.jpg) — near-white bleached oak with grey pores, soft cathedrals and fine cracks, matt (not lacquered in Сорренто) — vertical on doors and sides, x on tops, drawer fronts, bed rails; provisional #e7e7e1.
