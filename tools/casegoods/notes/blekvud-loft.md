## Construction (from the hall instructions, applied to the whole collection)
- Carcass ЛДСП **25** «Дуб Вотан»: top and bottom over the full L × B, sides B − 2 deep between them, 2 mm in from the
  ends — every inner panel is L − 54 (704 → 650, 904 → 850, 504 → 450; the tables prove it).
- Backs ДВП laminated white, in grooves of the sides (+5 a side) and top / bottom (+4…5); split backs meet on a
  128 mm rail «брусок» (3.31) or on a fixed shelf (3.35: 516 + 472 = 980 + 8).
- Inner panels ЛДСП 16 oak: fixed shelves 359 deep behind the fronts (z 23.5…382.5), loose shelves 339.
- Fronts ЛДСП 16 **inset** flush with the sides; 2 mm round, 3–4 between (3.31: 321 + 3 + 321 in 650; 1726 in 1730).
  Two decors: «Черный 660 WML» and oak. Wardrobe doors are three panels joined on dowels — oak 698 / black 280 /
  oak 748 (3.31, parts 9 + 15 + 5), the same scheme scaled on 1.27–1.29 (band 552…1060, read off the photo) and on the
  coupe doors.
- Drawers (3.35): the black front IS the box's front wall (Rastex to the sides), sides 16 oak 300 × 96, back 792.6 × 77
  standing on the ДВП bottom (804 × 305, in grooves of the sides and 5 mm in the front), ball runners 300 (12.7 mm a side).
  The other chests use the same box scaled to their fronts (side 300–400, height front − 45).
- Legs «Опора угловая 120 мм»: black steel corner legs — 25 mm post in each corner flush with the carcass, two 85 mm arms
  under the bottom (drawings of 3.31 / 3.33 / 3.35, photo 3.33). Wardrobes 1.27–1.29 stand on 15 mm glides; the coupes
  on their sides and a plinth.
- Handles «OZKM 5132» black: flat bar on two posts, ~150 long (3.33 photo: 143; 3.35 drawing: 146) → `bar` d 150, band 10,
  t 7, square posts 9, standoff 24.
- Height checks: 120 + 25 + 1730 + 25 = 1900 (3.31), 120 + 25 + 980 + 25 = 1150 (3.35), 120 + 25 + 280 + 25 + 30 cushion
  = 480 (3.33).
