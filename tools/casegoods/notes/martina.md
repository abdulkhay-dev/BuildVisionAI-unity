## Construction (by catalogue)
- Carcass ЛДСП 16 on a plinth 95 with bracket feet (an ogee cut-out between the end feet, `shape: path`) and a silver bead;
  pilasters 80 (МДФ 22) at the front corners with carved lilies at the top and foot; fronts inset between them, flush;
  mullions 40 between the columns of the tumbas.
- Low pieces: a 28 top 22 mm out with a silver edge line, over a frieze 40 with a silver rope bead; tall pieces (vitrines,
  wardrobe): a frieze 70, a crown 60 high 30 mm out (`martina-crown`), a cap.
- Doors: a milled frame 70 round a carved panel (the leaves relief as milled V-lines on a panel of role `carve`, the
  silver ground) with a bead and a silver patina line; drawers and the wardrobe's upper panels: a frame face with a bead;
  glazed doors: frame 70, clear glass, glazing bead. Silver knobs Ø28; the wardrobe has vertical silver bars.
- 01 / 01-1: glazed door over a carved door 690, hinged right / left (as the p. 48 knobs show); 02: two of each; 22 / 23:
  a row of drawers 150 over carved doors; 09: carved door | niche over a drawer | carved door; 50: wall shelf (back board,
  shelf, apron); tables 54 (built closed), 31, 32: top 30 over an apron with the rope bead, square legs with lilies;
  1.02 wardrobe: outer doors plain upper panel over a carved lower one, the middle door a mirror over a carved panel, left
  two sections hang, right one shelves (the p. 48 sketch); 1.03: niche over a carved door; 1.04 / 1.05 chests: a plain top
  drawer over carved drawers; 1.06 mirror: pilasters, a frieze with lilies, a small crown; 1.01 bed: an arched headboard
  (`shape: path`) with a carved band under the arch, rails and foot rail on block feet 150 (p. 47), metal base.

## Limits (for the lead)
- The leaves carving, the lilies and the gilded ornaments need a `print` / relief texture: there is no library material
  for them, so they are milled lines on grey plates (roles `carve`), listed in martina_decors.md.
- The rope moulding is a plain half-round bead (a twisted profile is not expressible).
