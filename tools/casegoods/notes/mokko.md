# «Мокко» (mokko, П7.053) — wave 5b

Only П7.053.1.10 «Шкаф-купе 2Д» 1536×610×2324 is in scope (catalogue p. 64: the cut-out, the interior scheme, the swatch
«Дуб Монтерей / Капучино», «внутри выдвижное зеркало»). No product page for 1.10, no instruction: **by catalogue**. Built
by `gen/globus.py` (`mokko_1_10`, the same coupe construction as Глобус); sheet `pilot/mokko-1-10.png` (ok; ref = the
cut-out, a 3/4 view — the meeting profile lands ≈ 30 mm left of the photo's because of the perspective).

## Construction
Carcass as Глобус (sides on the floor, top on them, bottom on a plinth 80, ХДФ back, partition at 846). Two sliding doors
106…2286 in graphite profiles, fillings «Капучино». Interior from the scheme: left section — top shelf 1990, rail 1865, a
**pull-out mirror** (a board 1190 × 460 with a mirror on its inner face, on runners fixed to the left side; `slide` 440
forward), a low shelf 460; right section — top shelf 1990, shelves 1640 / 1265, four drawers 1030–1245 … 429–632 with small
bar handles (the scheme draws dark pulls), a shelf 425 under them.

## Finish
`mokko-monterey-kapuchino`: body «Дуб Монтерей» #747474 (left half of the p. 64 swatch, crop 0.65,0.865,0.672,0.905),
front «Капучино» #a9a7a7 (right half, crop 0.683,0.865,0.705,0.905) — the cut-out shows the darker oak carcass and the light
plain doors. Monterey is textured (provisional, `gen/mokko_decors.md`); Капучино is plain. Metal `gold#5c5e61` (the
graphite profiles, cut-out crop reads #535558).

## For the lead
The scheme is a ≈ 60 × 80 px bitmap: shelf heights ±50 mm; a small item in the middle of the left section (a trouser
hanger or a tie rack?) was skipped. The mirror's real mechanism (a vertical pull-out on a top runner, it may also turn) is a
plain slide.
