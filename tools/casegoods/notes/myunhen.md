# «Мюнхен» (myunhen, П3.983) — wave 5b

Catalogue pp. 138–139 (printed 272–275): interiors with 4.02 («Дуб Онтарио») and 4.01 («Сосна Карелия»), cut-outs folded /
unfolded of all three codes, five decors. Site: two 3/4 photos of each. No instructions: all three **by photo**, inner sizes
±30 mm (3/4 views only). Generator `gen/myunhen.py`; sheets `pilot/myunhen-*.png` (ok; refs = the 3/4 site photos, rough).
Chairs of the pages skipped.

## The three codes (they differ in the pedestal only)
- **4.01** — V: two slanted leg blocks 160 wide × 100 deep (13.7°, meeting at the bottom), a back spine board between them,
  a stadium-shaped plate 840 × 560.
- **4.02** — X: seven layered boards 16 (four full X boards and three set back 15 mm between them — the dark grooves on
  the photos, 112 deep), a rectangular plate 820 × 520.
- **4.03** — trapezoid box: front and back panels 500 wide at the plate, 800 at the top (they run up to the top as the
  apron's long boards), slanted side boards (`rot` about z 11.5°), a plate 800 × 500 with R 60 corners.
The page's «Крашение» (Молоко, Дуб рустикаль, Сантана) belongs to the chairs: the tables are ЛДСП in one decor throughout
(site: «Каркас ЛДСП, Столешница ЛДСП»; the photos).

## Common

ЛДСП 16 800 × 460 × 80; the insert 400 × 800 is a butterfly leaf stored folded (two 400 × 400 halves) in the apron and
