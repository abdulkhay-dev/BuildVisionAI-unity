## How this wave was made (read before rendering)

- **No network to pinskdrev.by / a.pinskdrev.ru** (blocked by the cloud environment's policy): no product photos, no
  instruction PDFs. The sources were the committed cut lists / page images and the catalogue PDF. Modules «by catalogue»
  were measured off the catalogue's cut-outs and photos with the collection's instructed modules (or, where a collection
  has none, the common Pinskdrev construction) as the model; their inner sizes are good to ±10–20 mm, the overall size is
  exact. Where the site is reachable later, the by-catalogue modules deserve a look against the product photos.
- **Checker:** `gen/check.py` = preview2d.py with the cut list from the committed JSON (`gen/cutlists/<id>.json` — the
  table completed from the page image — else `reference/<slug>/cutlists/<id>.json`). Two differences from preview2d:
  1. the catalogue depth B is the carcass / top depth and **handles stand out of it** (Рокси 0.01: sides 383 + front 19 +
     gap = top 404 = B; Скай: sides = top = 420 = B; Шарли: 19 + 366 + 19 + 3 = top 407 = B) — an extent error caused only
     by handles is printed as a «примечание»; preview2d will report these as errors;
  2. a part with `shape: path` is tested for overlaps by its outline (notched bottoms round corner stiles, curved legs).
  Also, preview2d draws a `bar` handle as a disc of diameter d and counts ±d/2 in the extent (false «примечание» lines on
  H / L on some modules), and does not draw `rod` parts (legs) or outlines. Several agents gave rod legs a bounding
  `box` so that the extent counts them — CaseBuilder.Rod ignores the box (it uses from / to), Bounds uses it.
- **Catalogue:** the fragments were merged with `gen/merge_catalog.py` (new entries appended, Flora untouched). Some
  generators (napoli.py, ken.py, triniti.py) rewrite their `gen/<slug>_catalog.json` when re-run: merge it again (an id
  whose content changed is reported).
- **Decors:** `decors.md` (Wave 1 section). All textured decors have provisional plain colours.

### Engine limits for the lead
- **No rotated boards.** The bed's upper headboard leans 8°. Its panels 1.1, 6 and 5 are written as `moulding`s with a
  straight path along x and a parallelogram section (profiles `triniti-hb-*`) to get the slope. As a result:
  - the pyramid relief of strip 5 is lost (faces work only on axis-aligned boards);
  - the checker cannot measure their sizes (they are `covers`);
  - preview2d draws them only as bands.

  A `tilt` / rotation on panels (or a face on mouldings) would express this properly.
- **Rods do not count toward the extent** (neither in preview2d nor in CaseBuilder.Bounds). The tapered splayed legs are
  `rod`s that also carry their bounding `box` (86×130×60, the cut-list size). The engine draws the rod from from/to and
  uses the box only for bounds. It would be cleaner if rods computed their own bounds.
- The rod's square section is not oriented, and its ends are cut square to the axis. At a 17° splay the top pokes about
  9 mm into the bottom panel and the foot is not flat on the floor. A "cut ends horizontal" option for legs would fix this.
- preview2d draws `shape: path` side panels (bed 7 / 8) as their boxes in the side view.

### Чего формат не выразил / ограничения
- Золотые полосы-вставки сделаны `rod` квадратного сечения (8 / 6 мм, на 2 мм над гранью): `check.py` не знает `rod`
  (не рисует и не считает в габарит); ножки комода/тумбы тоже `rod` (кронштейн + конический столбик), под ними
  `tube`-подпятник 14 мм, чтобы габарит H доходил до пола. Нужна бы деталь «инкрустация» (полоса металла в пазу фасада).
- Ручка-скоба П-образная передана `bar` (планка на двух стойках) — близко; на листах check.py `bar` рисуется кругом.
- Рамки спинок на фото с небольшим уступом/фаской — переданы плоскими накладками.
- Велюр «мятый» — только плоский цвет, пока нет декора.
- Референсы: шкаф, комод, зеркало — вырезки анфас (линии сходятся); тумбы и кровати — перспектива (грубо). Вырезка шкафа
  по ширине/высоте даёт разный масштаб (2.24 и 2.37 мм/пкс) — картинка в каталоге чуть сжата по высоте; размеры по
  горизонтали брал в горизонтальном масштабе.
