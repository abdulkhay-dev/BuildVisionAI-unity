## Марлен (marlen) — 6 модулей, все по каталогу (генератор gen/marlen.py)

**Sources.** Catalogue p. 105 only — one bedroom interior (комод, стол, шкаф комбинированный, шкаф угловой, кровать,
тумба) seen in perspective; the collection has no module cut-outs, no close-ups and no swatches, and index.json has no
instructions. Every size inside the modules is read off that photo with the catalogue size as scale (±15–20 mm); the
overall sizes are the catalogue's (all confirmed on the page text).

**Construction:** carcass ЛДСП 16 «Гикори Кингстон» on 12-mm glides: sides from the glides to under the top, the top
over them, the bottom between them just above the floor (its oak edge shows under the fronts, as on the chest), ХДФ back
in grooves. Fronts ЛДСП 16 «Персидский жемчуг», inset between the sides / top / bottom and flush with the carcass edges,
2–3 mm gaps. Handles: long satin-aluminium bars 22 + 10 out of the face (outside B = the carcass): 320 horizontal on the
chest, desk and bedside drawers, 272 on the wardrobe drawers, 336 upright on the doors near the meeting / free edge.
- комод 1.25: three equal drawers, handles near the top of each front.
- тумба 1.21: a drawer (≈ 198) under an open niche; the shelf between them flush with the sides.
- стол 1.22: side panels 25 set in 20 under a 25 top (the top overhangs the sides and the fronts by 20), two drawers side
  by side on a drawer shelf with a partition, a modesty panel at the back.
- шкаф комбинированный 1.23: two doors (1641) over two drawers (287 / 310); inside a partition, three shelves on the left,
  rail on the right, hat shelf.
- шкаф угловой 1.24 (900 × 579): read as a blind-corner unit — on p. 105 it stands in the corner at right angles to the
  combined wardrobe, which covers the left part of its front; only a pearl door on the free end and the oak end side are
  seen. Built with a fixed oak blind panel on the left half and a door (hinged left, handle at the free edge) on the
  right half, hat shelf, rail and shelves across. This interpretation is the weakest point of the collection — an
  L-shaped corner unit is possible; please check against a product photo when the site is reachable.
- кровать 1.20: oak headboard 25 with an upholstered panel of six vertical channels (`soft`, 80 thick) over the upper
  part, rails and foot 25 (230 high) on four round tapered oak legs 190 (Ø50 → 34), cleats, slats, a middle beam,
  mattress 2000 × 1600.

**Finish:** one option «Персидский жемчуг» / «Гикори Кингстон»: `front` door_enamel_whitey#dfe3e2 (p. 132 swatch, the
lead's reference; the p. 115 «Вена» chart prints #d8d3cc), `body` provisional #a2886f = the «Гикори Кингстон» swatch on
p. 115 («Вена», Каркас) — decor listed in gen/marlen_decors.md. Metal `chrome#bdbcc0` = the handles (p. 105, #c0b8c5).
Headboard fabric: library default (Linen), colour ≈ #c9bcab on the photo.

**For the lead:**
- preview2d draws a `bar` handle as a circle of its length `d` and counts a horizontal bar ±d/2 vertically in the extent
  (hence the "примечание" lines about H / L and the big grey discs on the sheets) — the engine builds real bars.
- Nothing skipped (no chairs / upholstered pieces in these two collections).
