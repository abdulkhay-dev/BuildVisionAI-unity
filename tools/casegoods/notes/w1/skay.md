## Скай (skay) — 4 модуля, все по инструкции

**Construction** (IS-P6-112-0-0x): top and bottom 16 run the full width and depth (420); the sides stand between them.
The carcass stands on gold metal tube legs «j» 180 mm (4 corners, plus the front middle on the 1492-wide units). All
fronts are ЛДСП 16 inset flush in the carcass (z 404–420) with 2.5–3 mm gaps. The partition closing a drawer or niche
column runs to the back (404); the one between two doors starts in front of the backs (388) and is covered by the doors.
Backs ХДФ 3.5 sit in grooves per section. Drawer boxes are ЛДСП 16 × 200 on 350 runners, 14 mm per side (13.5 on
0.02 / 0.03). Handles are gold round knobs Ø30.

**Legs:** the instruction drawings show legs of ~150–160 mm. The catalogue heights (610, 942, 1492, 2020) all give
exactly 180 over the carcass, so 180 is used and the overlays are aligned on the carcass.

**Cut lists:** rows missing from the PDF text were added from the page images: 0.02 row 12 (back 408 × 499), 0.03 rows
6 (partition 730 × 404) and 9.1 (door 726 × 490.5), 0.07 row 7 (shelves 463 × 378 ×4). In 0.01, parts 1–2 are the sides
and 3–4 the top and bottom (the other modules number them the other way round).

**Finish:** «Базальт 683 PO» #4d4e49, the swatch on p. 115 (Вена chart; Скай has none). Metal gold#b39a5e from the
knobs / legs in the p. 25 photo.
