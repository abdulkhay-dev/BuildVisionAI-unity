# Brief for door design authors (waves 2–5)

You author door designs of the DveriMebel / el'PORTA / BRAVO catalogue (2020) for "House", a Unity 6 app that builds
houses from JSON. Doors are generated by a C# engine from your JSON. The user wants the doors **one to one** with the
catalogue. Repo: /Users/abdulxay/Documents/works/ansormed/unity. Do NOT edit C#, `catalog.json`, `external.json`, other
authors' files or existing designs unless your task says so.

## Method (as wave 1 did: every line within ~1 px of the big photos)
1. Measure with `tools/doors/measure.py` (bands, overlay) and look at the photos (Read tool on zoomed crops).
2. Write the design; check with `tools/doors/preview2d.py <design> --photo … --check` and look at the overlay
   (photo | design | overlay). Iterate until the lines land. Use every photo of a model (all colours, big and small).
3. Check it in 3D with the real generator (Unity is running; tool `mcp__unityMCP__execute_code`):
   `return House4696.Doors.DoorPreview.RenderDesign("/abs/path/design.json", "<finish id>", "<glass id or null>", "/abs/out.png", "front");`
   Views: "front" (catalogue view, 0.4 px/mm, same scale as the big photos), "back", "angle", "detailTop". Optional
   arguments: leafW, leafH, open, kind ("swing" / "sliding" / "folding"), leaves, block ("t70", "t70-2", "t70-3",
   "classic", "classic-rosette"). A finish id of the shipped catalogue shows its texture; a finish that is not
   imported yet falls back to a plain colour — fine for geometry. Put the render next to the photo and compare.
   Curves and parts (`groove`, `inlay`, `glass` with `bead`, `panel`, `molding`, `piece`) are only drawn by the 3D
   generator, not by preview2d — check those in 3D.
4. Resizing: the same design must look right at 600, 700, 800, 900 mm (fixX / fixY for stiles and rails, the thin
   rule keeps strips and narrow inserts). Render 600 and 900 once per model.

## Output
- `Assets/House4696/Resources/Doors/Designs/<design-id>.json` — one per unique design (the same design sold in several
  series or finishes is ONE file). Ids: latin kebab-case from the catalogue name (`trend-21`, `legno-38`, `glace-1`,
  `laminated-4g`…). Coordinates in mm as measured (not rounded to "nice" numbers without evidence).
- `Assets/House4696/Resources/Doors/Series/<series-id>.json` — one per catalogue series you cover: a single object
  `{ "id", "name", "line", "kind": "interior", "block", "order": <catalogue page>, "finishes": [...], "glass": [...],
  "models": [ { "id", "name", "design", "glass": [...], "finishes": [...] (only when the model is sold in fewer
  colours than the series), "photo": "<best photo file>" } ] }`. A model id is what a house names; the same model in
  another series (EcoShpon and EuroShpon LEGNO) keeps the same id and design.
- Previews in `tools/doors/.cache/preview/<design-id>.png` and a short report: per model what the design contains,
  measurements that matter, decisions and uncertainties, missing finishes / glass.
