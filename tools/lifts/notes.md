Source: `~/Downloads/glz2024catalog.pdf` (ООО «Гомельский лифтовый завод», 2024). 25 PDF pages, each a spread of two
catalogue pages; the catalogue number is printed bottom-right as `NN/MM`. PDF page index `i` (0-based) = catalogue
pages `2i-5 / 2i-4`.

`python tools/lifts/extract_refs.py <pdf>`). All sizes in mm, exactly as printed.

## Legend (same in all tables)

Q load kg · V speed m/s · JJ door clear width · AA×BB×CH car inside (width × depth × height) · AH×BH shaft clear
(width × depth) · AM×BM machine room · OH headroom (top landing floor → shaft ceiling) · PD pit depth · TR travel ·
H/HT shaft height · HM machine-room height · HH landing door height · CO centre-opening, 2S two-speed side-opening.

## Where the car sits in the shaft (from the plans; drawings are schematic, not to scale)

General for all: landing door is on the shaft **front wall**, the door opening (JJ) is centred on the **car**
centreline; car rails are on the two side walls at mid-depth of the car (the car-rail axis goes through the car
centre); the sill/door zone is in front of the car (≈ 40–45 % of BH−BB), the rest is behind.

- **K600 MR rear CW** (`drawings/k600-mr-rear.png`): car **centred** left-right in the shaft, door centred on the
  shaft axis. Counterweight frame **behind the car**, centred, against the rear wall. Rough split of BH−BB (650):
  ~365 behind (CW zone), ~285 in front (door zone). Shaft-plan depth is mislabelled BM (it is BH).
  Machine room above the shaft, AM×BM = AH×BH, clear height ≥2650, an opening marked **800×2000** on the MR side wall
  (only plan where the MR door is not ≥1200×2100). Section wall marks: 300 / 2500 / 2500 / 700 (top→pit floor).
- **K600 MR side CW** (`k600-mr-side.png`): CW on the left side wall (catalogue says right/left = mirror), car
  **offset away from the CW**: CW-side gap ≈ 60 % of AH−AA, opposite gap ≈ 40 %. Door centred on the car.
  MR door **≥1200×2100** in the MR front wall. MR height ≥2650. Wall marks: 500 / 2500 / 2000 / 400.
- **K600L MRL** (`k600l-mrl.png`): machine on a beam in the headroom, no MR. Two plans:
  upper — CW in the rear-left corner (bracket), car close to centre, CW-side gap ≈ 65 % of AH−AA;
  lower — CW beside the car on the left with an angled machine, car offset right, CW-side gap ≈ 75 %.
  Table ↔ plan mapping is NOT printed (my guess: table 1 = upper, table 2 = lower). Wall marks 300 / 2500 / 700.
- **K600G MR / MRL** (panoramic; `k600g-mr.png`, `k600g-mrl.png`): rectangular shaft with a glazed **bay protruding
  from the rear wall** — semicircular (半圆) or trapezoidal "rhombus" (菱形). BB/BH are measured to the apex of the
  bay. CW on the left side wall, car offset right; door on the front wall centred on the car. MR: height ≥2300,
  no MR plan but AM×BM given. Rear wall: solid only up to 1400 above the pit floor (MR) / partial (MRL), glass above.
  MRL wall marks ≥300 / 2500 / 800.
- **H700 MR 2:1 / 4:1** (`h700-mr-2-1.png`, `h700-mr-4-1.png`): CW on the left side wall, car **nearly centred**
  (slightly toward the right; heavy rails/brackets on the right too). Door centred on the car; JJ = AA for the
  narrow cars, JJ < AA for wider ones. 2:1: MR plan with a double swing MR door **≥1200×2100**; HM, door height
  arrow without numbers. 4:1: two shaft plans (single vs heavier CW frame on the left).
- **H700 car lift 2:1 / 4:1** (`h700-car-*.png`): CW on the left side wall, car almost centred (≈ equal gaps),
  three rail brackets on the right wall. MR door **≥1400×2100**. HH (landing door height) drawn, no number.
- **H700 MRL 2:1 / 4:1** (`h700-mrl-*.png`): CW on the left; 2:1 has diagonal rope/beam layout and car offset right;
  4:1 car nearly centred. The top "plan" is the headroom/machine plan and carries AM/BM labels that the table lacks.

Door heights: no drawing gives a number for HH; cabin height CH is 2385/2490 (passenger), 2400/2385 (K600G),
2300/2600 (freight), 2200/2400 (car lift). Machine-room doors: K600 MR side ≥1200×2100, H700 MR 2:1 ≥1200×2100,
car lift ≥1400×2100, K600 MR rear 800×2000 (as printed).

`topClearance` / `pitBottomGap` in the JSON are the top-most and bottom-most numbers on the shaft wall in the section
(K600: 300/700, 500/400; K600L 300/700; K600G 300/1400, 300/800). They are NOT door-related: they look like the
spacing marks of rail-bracket/ring-beam embeds (middle marks 2500 / 2000 are listed in each model's note).
H700 sections have no numbers (all null).

## Data decisions

- One row per car size. Where a cell spans several car sizes (OH/PD per speed, AH/AM shared), it is copied to each.
- H700 MR 2:1, 1600 & 2000 kg: the table repeats both car sizes under 0.5 and under 1.0 m/s with identical numbers →
  merged to one row per car size with `speeds: [0.5, 1.0]`.
- K600L second table: columns are labelled **AS / BS** but the values (3800–4000, 1450–1550, rising with speed) are
  OH / PD → stored as overhead/pit in model `k600l-mrl-b`.
- K600 MR rear: no door-type column → `doorType: null` (plans show centre-opening). Same for K600L, K600G, H700.
- `machineRoom` = null where the table has no AM/BM (K600 MR side, all MRL).
- Panoramic `shape`: 半圆 → `semicircle`, 菱形 → `rhombus` (the drawn bay is a trapezoid).
- Finishes: one id per look (20 ids). Cabin `wallDecor` keeps the decor sheet number / secondary finish of a wall.
- Items referenced by cabins but not pictured in the catalogue (ceilings SL-2036, 2017, 2078TJ, 2113ME, 2226J, 2039BJ,
  SLD-610J, 2240, 2238, 2233; handrails 3501TJ, 3301ME, 3101J, 3302BE, 3703, 3701; floors 4028P, 4017P, 4012P,
  4036P, 4072D, 4150D, 4082D, 4039D) are included with `pictured: false`, described from the cabin photo, `image`
  pointing at that cabin photo.
- Colour hex = mean of the inner 80 % of the printed swatch (CMYK→RGB render, so only approximate).
- Cabin photos are the embedded images at native resolution (433×679 … 661×855). Panoramic cabins (embedded only
  ~256 px wide) and SL-1156 (composite) are clip-renders at 250 dpi.
- `plain-stainless` landing door = photo from freight SL-1146 (p.36), used as the standard stainless landing door;
  `plain-stainless-4p` (SL-1156, 4-panel centre-opening) and `painted-b531p` (SL-1152) are the freight ones.

## Anomalies kept as printed (possible catalogue typos)

1. K600 MR rear, 1250 kg, 1.75 m/s: OH **4660** (1350 kg row and neighbours: 4650).
2. K600 MR rear, 1250 kg 1.6 m/s PD 1600 while 1000 kg 1.6 → 1500 (fine, just irregular).
3. K600 MR rear, 1000 kg 1100×2100 car: BH−BB = 400 only (other rows 650) although CW is at the rear.
4. K600 MR side: 2S shafts are narrower than CO (1900 vs 2100; 2300 vs 2500) — usually the reverse.
5. K600L table 1: 1350 kg AH **3050** > 1600 kg AH 3000 (re-checked at 300 dpi: printed so).
6. K600G MRL 1000 kg semicircle BH **2450**; rhombus and the MR version 2420.
7. H700 MR 4:1, 3000 kg: JJ **1500** with a 2100-wide car (2:1 version JJ 2100).
8. H700 MRL 2:1, 2000 kg: AH **2850** < 1600 kg AH 2900, OH 4450 < 4500.
9. H700 car lift 2:1: car height CH 2200, the 4:1 one 2400.
10. Colour "535P" printed without a letter prefix (others B…P).
11. Ceiling of SL-621 is **SLD-610J**, the ceiling list shows **SLD-600J** (similar look).
12. SL-1109 photo shows rose-gold front wall; text says front wall/door "нержавеющая сталь".
13. SL-1136 "smoky-grey" stainless renders as dark bronze-brown in the photo.
