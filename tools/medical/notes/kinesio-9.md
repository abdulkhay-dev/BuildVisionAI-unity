# kinesio-9 + kinesio-10 — exercise / OT equipment (19 devices)

Generators: `tools/medical/gen/k9_*.py`. The helpers are in `k9lib.py`:
- the kit of the hydraulic exerciser series (cream tube, light-blue pads, black caps);
- seat, backrest, roller, grip, hydraulic cylinder and logo helpers;
- `crop_screen`, which lays a screen crop so that its UI area lands on a given rectangle and masks the margins with a
  holed plate 2 mm in front.

`k9_handtable.py` is the shared body and stations of XY-101C and JY-CT-II.

For each device, the parts list was written from the photo before drawing. The final check was done against
`tools/medical/renders/<id>-compare.png`, using the last render of the last edit.

Frame: the patient's front is +z (z = depth), and +x is the patient's LEFT.

## Hydraulic exerciser series (xy-dssz-*, xy-dsxb-*, xy-dsxz-*): common kit
Taken from the 9 leaflet photos and the group photo:
- **Frame:** cream powder-coated round steel tube Ø50 (#f1e9d3). Every floor tube ends in a big black rubber cap
  (Ø64 × 58).
- **Pads:** light-blue vinyl (#a6c8e8) in three layers: a blue base, a light pinkish-grey piping band (#e6e1dc) and a
  thicker blue top, with rounded corners.
- **Grips and cylinders:** black foam grips Ø40–46. Black hydraulic cylinders (Ø46 body plus a chrome rod).
- **Logo:** a white Xiangyu logo on the upper backrest or the pads. It is drawn as a mark disc with a cross, 翔宇 in
  stroke letters, blocks for 医疗 and a line for the English text, so it is approximate.
- **Seat:** top ~500.

No sizes are printed. The inventory estimates were checked against the photos' proportions, using the ~400-wide seat
as the scale, and all were kept.
