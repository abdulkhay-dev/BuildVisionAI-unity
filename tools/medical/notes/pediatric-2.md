## Final check (last edit of every device confirmed on a fresh `renders/<id>-compare.png`, all `ok`)

Engine notes learned here: `wood#` lays its grain along y on front faces and along z on top faces, so boards running
along x are drawn turned 90° (`wbox` in `pd2_lib.py`; slats of xyrt-17/18). `copies` offsets do not follow a tilted
part (separate parts used on the xyrt-14 top); `sphere` ignores `rot` (lathe used instead). A lathe ring must be
listed in the right order or it renders inside-out (trampoline covers).
