## Results
All 14 render `ok`. Each was checked part by part against its photo(s) (angle/front/side renders); 2–4 rounds each.

### Engine findings (for whoever touches MedBuilder or writes designs)
- `decal` ignores `copies`, `repeat`, `mirror` and `rot`: Decal() draws into the decal builder without the part
  transform, so only the first copy appears, unrotated. Use thin boxes for repeated marks (lettering, dot fields).
- `cyl` ignores `rot` (the radiator wires of xy-ppzl-ii stayed upright). `bar`, `box`, `lathe`, `slab`, `screen` honour
  it. For tilted rods I compute the end points myself (`rotx` in p1lib.py), with copy offsets rotated too.
- A straight `bar` laid on a curved lathe surface sinks in the middle and stands off at the ends. Use a `tube` through
  several points on the surface.
- `screen` prints stretch the whole screenCrop over the face. Where the crop also shows the casing (xy-k-medical,
  xy-fswt-ic), I sized the screen box to the panel plus its rim and used the casing material, so the white corners of
  the crop merge with the body.
