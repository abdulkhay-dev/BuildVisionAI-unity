## Results and final check (every device renders `ok`)
Compare sheets were built with ImageMagick: `compare.py` needs PIL, which none of the local pythons have. The sheets are
the same layout and land at the same `renders/<id>-compare.png` path.
Watch for this frame trap: `mirror: "x"` mirrors about the middle of the SIZE width. On devices whose body is off-centre
(the arms widen the size), mirrored parts land in the air. The generators use explicit `copies` there.
