# Detail review: batch sensory-1 (14 devices)

Each device was compared against its catalogue photo, enlarged 2–4× in the scratch folder (some with a pixel ruler), and against the
angle, front and side renders. Generators: `gen/s1lib.py` (shared panel case), `gen/s1_panels.py`, `gen/s1_jy_tyhd.py`,
`gen/s1_misc.py`. Only this batch's ids were regenerated. All 14 renders are `ok` and newer than their json.

## Shared panel case (cognitive / colour-conversion / dynamic-wheel / endless-mirror / fibre wall)
- Ear speaker grilles: these were flat darker discs. The photos show round grilles of small holes, so they are now rows of 4 mm dots
  filling a 60 mm circle, in a darker tone of the case.
- Logo: the characters were bright green, but the photo shows dark green / near black, so they are now #1d5a32. The leaf mark is now
  round, not a square.

## cognitive-training-board
- Display content was wrong. The model had a black screen with a thin tan line and one column of cards. The photo shows:
  - a golden "bamboo" background over the whole display;
  - a green title banner with yellow text;
  - a peach column of 2 × 7 white cards;
  - the red-framed white page;
  - 5 pink tabs on the right;
  - 4 green oval buttons on a dark strip under the page;
  - a small purple corner mark.
  All of these are now drawn.
- Pot painting: the olive-brown pot with green leaves, the white figure, the green cluster and the title are now drawn. The pot and
  the buttons are raised above the decal layers, because at first they were hidden under them.
- Case, ears, whiskers, the paw-dot pattern, speakers, lines and buttons were checked and match.
- Format limit: there is no screenCrop, so the interface is made of flat decals, not the real picture.

## color-conversion-control-panel
- The photo shows a small black port low on the x = 0 side; it has been added.
- The red push button was too bright. It is now darker red (#a8182a), as in the photo.
- The positions and directions of the triangle keys were checked against the 3/4 photo and match: red ▶ yellow ▲ blue ◀ /
  pink ▶ yellow ▲ green ◀, and the D-pad.

## dynamic-color-wheel
- The windmill turned the wrong way, mirroring the photo. Seen from the front, the photo's arms curve clockwise going out; the model's
  curved counter-clockwise. This is fixed.
- The arms were thin and uniform. They are now thick deep-cyan arms that open into light-cyan fans.
- Solid pale-blue discs were replaced by a lobed (8-lobe) disc of faint dot lines with a white centre, as in the photo.
- Format limit: the dots are drawn as fine strokes.

## endless-depth-light-mirror
- The model had solid concentric pink rings that the photo does not have. They were replaced by dotted rays that radiate from each
  white circle: strong pink near the circle, fading outwards, and clipped where they meet the other circle.
- The figure "8" was re-measured on the photo: circle centres at y 541 / 378, with slightly flattened white circles. The red waist
  rays and the ellipse were kept.

## fiber-optic-curtain-wall
- The fibre field had 12 thin lines and read like ruled paper. It now has 20 thicker wavy grey strands, and the faint pink glow along
  the field's edges seen on the photo.
- The raised lip now ends at y 116, as on the photo (it was at 93).
- Format limit: the soft, fuzzy glow of real fibres can only be approximated with strands.

## butterfly-fiber-falls
- Body colours were the wrong way round. The model had a solid yellow body with green stripes; the photo shows a green body drawn
  with yellow lines. It now has a yellow-ringed head with a flower of 5 dots and 2 feelers, a yellow body outline, 5 yellow chevrons
  and a yellow tail point.
- Outline and positions were re-traced on the photo about the body's centre line:
  - rounder upper-wing lobes, with the V notch between them;
  - a waist notch on each side;
  - a tail lobe at the bottom;
  - wings, spots, head and antennae moved down to the photo's proportions (head at about 0.63 of the height, not 0.73).
- The fibre sheath had kinks at its bends. It is now a smooth Bezier curve to the left.
- Format limit: the photo is a 3/4 view, so the outline is accurate only to a few cm. The sheath below the photo's edge goes to the
  floor, as the inventory text says.

## jy-tyhd-i
- Mesh band: the plain grey band now has the photo's diagonal mesh hatch, clipped to the band. The ▼ marker was solid white; it is
  now a white outline with a grey inside, as in the photo.
- Eye speakers: these were plain flat discs. They now have a thick dark rim and a mesh of dark holes, and were reduced from Ø136 to
  Ø124 to match the photo.
- Bay: the middle plate is now light grey (it was white, the same as the bay). The diamond is darker. The small grey sticker on the
  bay's left was added.
- Side check: the vent grid and the black slot are on the x = 0 side, as on the photo. The angle render shows the other side.
- Format limit: the castors are the engine's castor, so they have no white wheel faces.

## led-piano-pedals
- Black keys: they read as mid-grey, because glossy and black-metal materials reflect the sky at grazing angles. They are now matte
  (fabric #060606) and read black. They were also narrowed from 84 to 60 mm, as on the photo.
- Key colours were pastel. They are now more saturated: violet, magenta, sky blue, orange, yellow, green, purple and lime, as on the
  photo.
- The layout was checked: the photo's near end matches x = 0, and the black keys start at the divider.

## lighting-color-changing-puzzle-table
- No differences were found at sheet scale: base, button order and colours, the orange acrylic tray, the glowing surface and the
  shape layout all match. No change.
- Format limit: the photo's translucent shapes are drawn as opaque gloss.

## multi-sensory-master-control-machine
- Side vents: the photo's "slanted" slots are horizontal louvres that only look slanted because of perspective. They are now 4 long
  horizontal slots stacked one above another. The port is now taller and narrower, near the front.
- Front door: re-measured as x 40–560, y 35–535. The handle is now the photo's dark "smile" slot at y ≈ 432, not a flat bar.
- Screen: the interface is now deep blue, with a light title line, a bright blue bar top left, a column of lighter tiles and darker
  panels. The base decal was hidden (coplanar) and now shows. The bezel is a dark bronze tone.
- Keyboard panel: faint keyboard keys were added under the screen.

## multi-sensory-matching-projection-system
- Front additions, all seen on the photo:
  - a dark horizontal-slat grille on the left;
  - a small silver badge left of the lens (it was a large white label);
  - the thin silver band along the bottom, with a white label.
- Lens: a grey outer lip, a dark inner ring and lighter blue-grey glass (it was dark blue), as on the photo.

## color-led-ball
- Facets: the mirror facets were a coarse 9 × 12 grid that read like a globe. They are now a 17 × 24 grid (about 40 mm tiles), so it
  reads as a mirror ball.
- LED cluster: the photo's cluster was hidden on the underside. It is now on the lower front, in a dark window that follows the
  sphere's surface (a lathe cap): an 18-LED ring, 6 middle LEDs and 3 bright centre LEDs, as in the photo.
- Format limit: the photo is only a close-up of the light, so the ball's body still follows the inventory text. The green colour of
  the photo comes from the room lighting and is not modelled.

## bean-bag
- Seat dent: there was none. The bag is now a low, wide body of 6 pinwheel gores, the same pole as before on the lower front-left. A
  thick horseshoe roll goes round the back and the sides; it is high at the back, sinks into the body at the front ends and leaves a
  seat dent at the top front. The roll is cut into pieces coloured by the gore they lie on, so the seams carry on over it: red back
  left, blue back right, yellow on the right, as on the photo's top.
- Size: 889 × 861 × 830.
- Format limit: this is a union of convex shapes, so the dent is a hollow between the roll and the body's low dome, not a carved
  hollow. The child's deformation is not modelled.
